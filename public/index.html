Why Jan-Setu?

Key interoperability principles enabling coordinated citizen governance without centralized databases.

1

One Citizen Request

Eliminates the friction of filing separate applications across disjointed department portals and physical offices.

2

Coordinated Verification

Orchestrates parallel automated queries across departmental endpoints, drastically reducing verification cycles.

3

Reduced Redundant Submissions

Citizens do not repeatedly upload the same identity or address proofs for every government interaction.

4

Schema Interoperability

Bridges differing departmental API response formats into a standardized, machine-readable composite record.

5

Data Minimization & Consent

Departments share only specific yes/no validation flags needed for the application—protecting citizen privacy.

6

Transparent Auditability

Every inter-departmental verification is recorded in an immutable audit log accessible to citizens and officers.

Architecture & Trust Model
"Departments retain ownership of their data. Jan-Setu requests only the information required for the current purpose."
Federated Governance Jan-Setu maintains no centralized citizen database. Department databases remain completely independent and self-managed.
Purpose Limitation Verifications are strictly bound to explicit application requests, preventing unauthorized cross-referencing.
Offline Queueing Resilience If a department service experiences simulated downtime, requests are queued safely and auto-processed upon recovery.
