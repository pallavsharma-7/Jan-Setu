Fault-Tolerance & Queue Resilience Simulation

Jan-Setu protects citizen workflows from downstream service outages by queuing requests gracefully rather than failing with 500 errors.

1
Simulate Outage:

Toggle any department (e.g. Tax Service) to OFFLINE. New or in-flight applications requesting that verification will transition safely into QUEUED state.

2
Resilient Holding:

Other available department checks continue to complete. Citizen is not asked to restart or re-submit documents.

3
Auto-Recovery Drain:

When the department returns ONLINE, Jan-Setu automatically resumes orchestration and drains the queue to completion.
