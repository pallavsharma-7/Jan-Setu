How Service Verification Works

When you submit an application for any service above, Jan-Setu does not copy entire departmental database records. Instead, it dispatches simulated, purpose-bound verification calls (e.g. POST /api/verify/identity, POST /api/verify/tax) directly to the relevant department modules.

If any department is experiencing simulated maintenance (e.g. status is offline), the Jan-Setu gateway automatically places that specific verification in a failure queue without losing your application data.
